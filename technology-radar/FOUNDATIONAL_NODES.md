## Interpretation

The ranking rewards cross-domain leverage and evidence, not novelty or media attention. It must be revised when new evidence changes a node's maturity, dependency centrality, or bottleneck status.

## Current high-confidence hypotheses

1. Compute scaling is constrained by a physical stack: memory, packaging, interconnect, thermal management, power delivery, and grid infrastructure.
2. Industrial autonomy requires measurement, semantics, digital twins, state estimation, verification, and deterministic control in addition to AI models.
3. Biology's commercialization bottleneck frequently moves from biological design into scale-up, process control, separation, quality, and manufacturing.
4. Critical minerals create leverage not only in mining but in refining, metallization, process equipment, magnet/material manufacturing, and recycling.
5. Simulation becomes more valuable when coupled to UQ, calibration, data assimilation, model reduction, inverse design, and automated experiments.
