# Interface Science — Cross-Domain Enabler

Status: STRONG_SIGNAL / RESEARCH_TARGET

## Thesis
Interfaces can become system-level bottlenecks even when the bulk materials or components are already adequate. Interface behavior couples chemistry, transport, mechanics, heat, electricity, optics, contamination and aging.

## Evidence patterns

### Semiconductor packaging
Hybrid bonding and advanced packaging expose yield, thermal, reliability, metrology and fine-pitch process constraints.

### Solid-state batteries
Recent 2026 reviews identify buried solid-solid interfaces as coupled chemical, electrochemical, mechanical and transport bottlenecks. Real contact area, interfacial reactions, stress and dendrite-related failure can determine cell performance. [E1][E2]

### High-voltage batteries
Cathode-electrolyte interfaces evolve under high voltage and drive degradation, impedance growth and reliability limits. [E3]

### Electrochemical systems
Electrocatalyst durability increasingly depends on surface/interface chemistry, adhesion, microenvironment and protection rather than activity alone. [E4]

### Membrane separation
Selectivity frequently comes from surface modification and interfacial nanoarchitecture; fouling and chemical stability remain central constraints. [E5][E6]

### Extreme environments
Protective coatings operate as engineered interfaces against oxidation, corrosion, wear, radiation, thermal shock and environmental attack. [E7]

## Why it matters to the 2026–2034 radar

An interface technology can unlock multiple domains because the same problem structure recurs:

```text
contact
  ↓
transport
  ↓
reaction / stress / heat / signal
  ↓
aging
  ↓
failure
```

## Research questions

1. Which interface properties dominate system failure?
2. Can interface behavior be measured in situ rather than after failure?
3. Can interfaces be actively controlled or self-heal?
4. Can interface engineering reduce material requirements?
5. Can interface behavior be simulated across scales?
6. Which interface processes lack scalable manufacturing equipment?
