## SRC-R03-01 — Solid-state battery interfaces
Source: Journal of Alloys and Compounds, 2026-07-15.
Title: A review of interfacial challenges and engineering strategies in solid-state batteries.

## SRC-R03-03 — Direct-to-package cooling
Source: Communications Engineering, 2026.
Title: Co-packaged electronics with microfluidics for direct-to-package cooling.
Observation: Embedded microchannels in package substrates reached about 625 W cm−2 in the reported test and reduced junction temperature/thermal resistance relative to air/heat-sink cooling; integration/fabrication is part of the challenge.
Use: Supports thermal-bottleneck migration and package-interface coupling.
URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44172-026-00620-9

## SRC-R03-04 — As-built digital twins and fatigue
Source: npj Metamaterials, 2026-06-02.
Title: Statistical average strain energy density fatigue estimation of strut-based metamaterials via synthetic as-built CAD digital twins.
Observation: Fatigue is strongly affected by manufacturing-induced geometric imperfections, porosity, surface roughness and localized stress concentrations; nominal CAD is insufficient for lifetime prediction.
Use: Supports as-built state and process-history-aware digital twins.
URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44455-026-00030-z
