# Open Questions — Research Queue

## Simulation / compute
- When do solver speedups stop mattering because model-form error dominates?
- Which preconditioners or multigrid techniques transfer best across domains?
- Can neural operators report reliable validity domains rather than only point predictions?
- How should uncertainty propagate across multi-fidelity and multiscale models?
- What is the minimum evidence package needed for a simulation result to become decision-grade?

## Materials / interfaces
- Which thermal interface materials have a credible manufacturing path at high volume?
- Which bonding/interconnect methods are likely to scale for heterogeneous 3D integration?
- Which surface treatments produce large lifetime gains at low process cost?
- Where are tribological limits likely to appear first in high-density robotics?

## Energy / minerals
- Which transformer design standardization measures have the largest capacity effect?
- Which power-electronics improvements remove actual grid bottlenecks?
- Which critical minerals are constrained primarily by process equipment rather than ore supply?
- Which by-product recovery routes can become economical under changing commodity conditions?

## Manufacturing
- Can in-situ metrology provide enough evidence for certification in high-value regulated production?
- What standards will become interoperability primitives across vendors?
- Can digital-twin composition preserve assumptions and validity across coupled twins?
- Can autonomous qualification be trusted without creating new hidden failure modes?

## Robotics
- Which sensing/actuation primitives matter more than a general-purpose humanoid form factor?
- How much tactile data is required for robust general manipulation?
- Can simulation plus state estimation close the sim-to-real gap for long-tail contact events?

## Biology
- What are the dominant economic bottlenecks in bioprocess scale-up by modality?
- How much can digital twins improve yield, contamination control, or scheduling?
- Which separation and downstream-processing technologies limit novel biological products?

## Water / agriculture
- Which selective-separation processes can combine water production with resource recovery?
- Can active membranes maintain durability and economics at field scale?
- Can direct plant-state sensing materially reduce water use outside controlled trials?

## Space / quantum
- Which autonomous PNT methods can become resilient enough for operational use?
- Can optical inter-spacecraft links provide both communications and navigation inputs at useful cost?
- Which quantum sensors reach ruggedized manufacture before fault-tolerant quantum computing reaches broad utility?

## Falsification targets
The project should actively search for evidence that:
- advanced packaging will cease to be a bottleneck earlier than expected;
- digital twins remain too costly or weakly validated for broad deployment;
- selective separation lacks sufficiently favorable economics;
- humanoid platforms outperform task-specific automation economically in realistic environments;
- simulation acceleration does not translate into faster physical discovery because experiment/qualification remains dominant.
